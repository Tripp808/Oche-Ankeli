Research

Building AI that can be evaluated in context.

My research asks how we can measure and improve the behavior of AI systems across languages, cultures, and real-world settings—especially where standard benchmarks leave important people out.

Research themes

These themes are connected by a commitment to meaningful evaluation: methods that are technically rigorous and accountable to the communities represented in the data.

Responsible AI & AI evaluation

Bias, fairness, safety, cultural relevance, and human-centered evaluation of large language models and AI products.

African & low-resource NLP

Dataset construction, multilingual modeling, and evaluation for languages and communities underrepresented in NLP.

Speech for health

Automatic speech recognition for African languages and maternal health, including domain adaptation and user testing.

Participatory methods

Community-centered collection, annotation, and multi-turn evaluation for systems intended for real people and institutions.
